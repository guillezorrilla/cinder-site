Cinder

Support

Cinder blocks your distracting apps until you touch a Tag. Touch it again to get them back.

Which Tags work?

NFC stickers or cards with an NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 chip. Cinder recognises a Tag by its serial number, so it doesn't need to write anything to it. Pair one from the Tags tab, or several: any paired Tag ends a Session.

My Tag isn't with me. How do I unblock?

Tap Emergency unblock on the Block tab. You get five, ever, and a reinstall doesn't reset them, so keep them for real emergencies. A Session started by a Schedule also ends on its own when the Schedule does.

Can I block without a Tag?

Yes. Press and hold Block for about a second. Ending that Session still needs a paired Tag or an Emergency unblock.

I lost my Tag

Use an Emergency unblock, then pair a new Tag in the Tags tab and remove the lost one.

Cinder stopped blocking

Cinder needs Screen Time access to block apps. If it was turned off in Settings, open Cinder and allow it again.
