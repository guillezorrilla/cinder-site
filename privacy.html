Cinder

Privacy Policy

Cinder collects no data. Everything it knows stays on your iPhone.

What Cinder keeps, and where

Your Modes, paired Tags, Schedules, Session history and Emergency unblock count are stored on your iPhone only. Cinder has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends anything over the network.

Screen Time

Cinder blocks apps with Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls). The apps and websites you choose are given to Cinder as opaque tokens: Cinder can block them but can't see which apps they are or how you use them.

NFC Tags

When you pair a Tag, Cinder saves its serial number (UID) on your iPhone so it can recognise that Tag later. It doesn't read or write anything else on the Tag.

Deleting your data

Deleting Cinder deletes its data. The one exception is the Emergency unblock count, kept in your iPhone's Keychain so a reinstall doesn't reset it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
